About Us

Built on Experience. Finished with Care.

Sharma Furniture is a furniture manufacturing and contracting business based in Bareilly, founded and led by Mr. Om Parkash Sharma, who brings 20 years of practical furniture industry experience.

From a single custom wardrobe to complete furniture work for a building, we focus on honest workmanship, customised solutions, professional execution and lasting customer satisfaction. We serve homeowners, builders, architects, civil engineers, developers and hotel owners across Bareilly.

Our Founder

Mr. Om Parkash Sharma

Mr. Om Parkash Sharma has spent approximately 20 years in the furniture industry, learning the craft through hands-on work on residential, commercial and hotel projects.

He understands furniture manufacturing, installation and project execution from the ground up. Today he brings that experience directly to clients under the Sharma Furniture name, with a personal commitment to quality and honest workmanship.
